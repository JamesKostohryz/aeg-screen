    Diff % is market price divided by screen value, minus one: negative means the market is below the screen's value. Sort any column by clicking it. AEG % is how much of the screen value is the abnormal-growth premium — the engine's full four-stage valuation of Microsoft puts that at 1.5%, so a row in the sixties is mostly a statement about the consensus estimate. Rows marked INPUT-DOMINATED (consensus growth above 30%, or abnormal growth above two-thirds of value in either direction) are hidden by default and never dropped.

    vs Group is this name's Diff % minus the median Diff % of its own risk group, so negative means cheaper than its peers. Cheap against Consumer Staples is a different claim from cheap against the index, and only the first is a claim about the company rather than about where defensives happen to be priced. The median is taken over unflagged rows only — one INPUT-DOMINATED degenerate would otherwise move a small group's benchmark more than anything real. Peers is how many companies went into that median, so it is a count and not an identifier: at two or three names it is a benchmark in name only. Use the dropdown to see one group at a time.

    Mkt PVGO % is the share of the price you pay that is growth not yet delivered: (price − Anchor) ÷ price, where Anchor is the no-growth value. This is the only column on the page that does not touch the consensus growth estimate — the anchor is built from normalized earnings, retention and the cost of equity, and the growth rate never enters it. So read it against 5y Growth: Mkt PVGO says how much growth the market is demanding, 5y Growth says how much consensus is supplying. A low PVGO with a high consensus rate is a different animal from the reverse.

    Anchor P/E, Fair P/E and Mkt P/E are the no-growth, screen-value and market multiples, all on the same normalized earnings, so the spread between them is the growth argument stated in multiples. Fair P/E +/- is Fair against Mkt — and it is worth saying plainly that this quantity collapses algebraically to Est. Value ÷ Market − 1, which is Diff % inverted. It is here because a P/E reads instantly, not because it is independent evidence. Nothing on this page is independent of the three numbers Anchor, Est. Value and Market.
